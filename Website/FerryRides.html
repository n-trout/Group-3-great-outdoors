Ferry Rides

Taste the Ocean

Explore the scenic ferry routes connecting Washington’s coastlines, islands, and waterfront towns.

  • 1. Seattle ↔ Bainbridge Island
    Duration: ~30–35 minutes
    Route: Jumbo Mark-II class auto/passenger ferries across Puget Sound
    Why go: Most heavily trafficked route (nearly 5 million passengers in 2024). Great for a day trip to Winslow’s shops, restaurants, art galleries, and wineries.
  • 2. Mukilteo ↔ Clinton (Whidbey Island)
    Duration: ~20 minutes
    Schedule: Runs daily from about 5 am to 1 am
    Scenery: Evergreen trees, Olympic Mountain views, and early morning coastal fog.
    On-island highlights: Ebey’s Landing, Langley art scene, Greenbank Farm.
  • 3. Seattle ↔ Bremerton
    Duration: ~60 minutes (state ferry)
    Plus: Passenger-only fast ferry by Kitsap Transit shortens the trip.
    Why go: A relaxing ride with water views, leading to Bremerton’s waterfront parks and naval history.
  • 4. Edmonds ↔ Kingston
    Duration: ~30 minutes
    Connection: SR-104 connects travelers across Puget Sound to the Kitsap Peninsula.
  • 5. Anacortes ↔ San Juan Islands (Orcas, Lopez, Friday Harbor)
    Duration: 1–2 hours
    Why go: A ferry ride through the Salish Sea to multiple islands. Permits cars and passengers. Feels like Alaska — without leaving Washington.
