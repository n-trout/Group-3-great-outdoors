Beaches

Relax in the Sun or Explore the Shore

Swim, sunbathe, picnic, or take in the ocean breeze at these popular beaches in the Pacific Northwest.

  • 1. Alki Beach Park (West Seattle)
    A half-mile stretch along Puget Sound featuring bike paths, volleyball nets, and casual eateries.
    Famously has a miniature Statue of Liberty. Great for a summer stroll or sunset view.
  • 2. Golden Gardens Park (Ballard)
    Known for soft sand, tide pools, colorful sunsets, and kite-flying. A local favorite on Puget Sound.
  • 3. Double Bluff Beach (Whidbey Island)
    About 1.5 hours from Seattle. Two-mile stretch with warm shallow waters, driftwood piles, and views of the Olympic Mountains.
  • 4. Third Beach (Olympic Peninsula near La Push)
    Reached by a 1.5-mile forest hike. Less-crowded beach with dramatic sea stacks and a beachside waterfall (Strawberry Bay Falls). Perfect for adventurous hikers and campers.
