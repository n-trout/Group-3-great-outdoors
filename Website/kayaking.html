Kayaking

Popular Kayaking Spots and Services in the Pacific West

Washington State

  • San Juan Islands – Known for calm waters and orca sightings.
    Guided tours: Discovery Sea Kayaks, San Juan Outfitters
  • Lake Union (Seattle) – Urban paddling with views of houseboats and the skyline.
    Rentals: Agua Verde Paddle Club, Northwest Outdoor Center
  • Deception Pass State Park – Beautiful but advanced spot with strong currents.
  • Moran State Park (Orcas Island) – Peaceful lake paddling surrounded by forest.

Oregon

  • Columbia River Gorge – Relaxing or challenging routes depending on location.
  • Hood River – Known for a mix of kayaking and windsurfing.
  • Alder Creek Kayak & Canoe (Portland) – Rentals, lessons, and tours for all levels.

Northern California

  • Russian River (Sonoma) – Calm, beginner-friendly waters through redwoods and wine country.
  • Tomales Bay (Point Reyes) – Peaceful paddling with wildlife spotting.
  • Klamath River – Recently restored, perfect for scenic nature kayaking.
