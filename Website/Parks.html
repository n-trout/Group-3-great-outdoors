Taste the Nature

Explore the stunning national parks of the Pacific Northwest—featuring glaciers, volcanoes, rainforests, and rugged coastlines.

  • Olympic National Park
    Ecosystems galore: Encompasses temperate rainforests, alpine peaks, glacier-fed rivers, and 60 miles of wild Pacific coastline.
    Highlights:
    • Hoh Rainforest – Lush, moss-draped forest with over 140 inches of annual rainfall.
    • Ozette Loop – 9-mile trail through cedar swamps and seaside forests (permits required).
    • Hurricane Ridge & Sol Duc – Mountain views, hot springs, waterfalls, lakes, and wildlife.
    Best for: Rich ecosystem diversity, coastal backpacking, rainforest hikes.
    Drive time: ~2–3 hours + ferry access for some coastal zones.
  • Mount Rainier National Park
    Signature landmark: 14,410 ft active stratovolcano, capped with glaciers and surrounded by wildflower meadows.
    Features:
    • Paradise and Sunrise areas – Scenic wildflower blooms, waterfalls, and mountain vistas.
    • Wonderland Trail – A 93-mile loop circling the mountain.
    • Mountaineering – About 10,000 summit attempts each year (~50% success rate).
    Best for: Mountaineering, alpine hiking, wildflower photography.
    Drive time: ~1.5 hours from Seattle/Bellevue.
  • North Cascades National Park
    “American Alps”: A rugged wilderness of jagged peaks, 300+ glaciers, and pristine lakes.
    Top spots:
    • Cascade Pass Trail – One of the region’s most iconic day hikes with dramatic views.
    • Diablo Lake Overlook – Famous for glacier-fed turquoise waters.
    Best for: Remote backpacking, alpine scenery, glacier viewing.
    Drive time: ~2–3 hours.
